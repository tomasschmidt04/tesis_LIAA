Funciones/scripts identificados
-------------------------------
- `build_precomputed_file_train_eval_datasets`: carga Train/Test JSONL precomputados y valida leakage por cuento.
- `build_measured_single_sentence_features`: tokeniza texto y prepara word ids para MLM/scanpath.
- `build_static_masked_inputs_and_labels`: genera inputs enmascarados y labels MLM.
- `train_step7_baseline`: loop de pretraining BETO solo con MLM estandar.
- `train_step7` en `train_mlm_combined_step7.py`: loop de pretraining BETO + scanpath.
- `compute_adaptive_scanpath_weight`: calcula lambda adaptativo.
- `select_scanpath_weight_update_metric`: elige eval/train scanpath loss para actualizar lambda.
- `Gazesup_BERTForCombinedMaskedLM.forward`: calcula `main_mlm_loss`, `scanpath_mlm_loss` y `total_loss`.
- `SP_Encoder._prepare_measured_word_scanpath` y `convert_word_pos_seq_to_token_pos_seq`: mapean scanpath palabra -> token/subtoken.
- `load_xnli_es_dataset`: carga XNLI espanol.
- `load_rioplatense_hate_binary_dataset`: carga CSVs de hate speech rioplatense.
